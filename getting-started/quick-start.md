# Quick Start

Get started with NavHub in 5 minutes!

## 1. Create Your Account

After installation, visit `http://localhost:8080` and create your first account:

1. Click "Register"
2. Enter your email and password
3. Verify your email (if configured)

## 2. Create Your First Page

Pages are containers for your widgets and bookmarks.

1. Click "New Page" in the sidebar
2. Enter a name (e.g., "Work", "Personal")
3. Choose a layout style
4. Click "Create"

## 3. Add Widgets

Widgets display information on your page:

1. Click the "+" button on your page
2. Browse available widgets:
   - **Clock** - Display time and date
   - **Weather** - Current weather conditions
   - **Bookmarks** - Quick links to your favorite sites
   - **RSS Feed** - News from your favorite sources
   - **Notes** - Quick notes and reminders
3. Click a widget to add it
4. Drag to reposition

## 4. Add Bookmarks

1. Click "Add Bookmark" or use the browser extension
2. Enter the URL
3. NavHub will auto-fetch the title and icon
4. Add tags for organization

## 5. Customize Your Dashboard

- **Themes** - Choose light or dark mode
- **Layouts** - Grid, list, or compact view
- **Colors** - Customize accent colors
- **Background** - Set a custom background image
