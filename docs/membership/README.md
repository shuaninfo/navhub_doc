## AI Tokens Explained

AI tokens measure usage of AI features:

- **Auto-categorization**: 100-200 tokens per bookmark
- **AI Summary**: 300-1000 tokens per page
- **AI Search**: 100-200 tokens per query
- **Translation**: 500-2000 tokens per page

### Token Limits

| Plan | Monthly Allocation | Rollover |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| Free | 10,000 | No |
| Pro | 100,000 | Up to 200,000 |

Pro users can accumulate unused tokens up to 200,000 total.

## Upgrading to Pro

Ready to upgrade? Here's how:

1. Go to **Settings → Account**
2. Click **Upgrade to Pro**
3. Choose your billing cycle
4. Enter payment details
5. Enjoy Pro features immediately

## Cancellation

You can cancel anytime:

1. Go to **Settings → Account**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Confirm cancellation

After cancellation:
- Pro features remain until period ends
- Data is never deleted
- Downgrade to Free plan automatically
- Can resubscribe anytime

## FAQ

**Can I try Pro before paying?**

Yes! Your Free plan includes 10,000 AI tokens to try Pro-level AI features. We occasionally offer free Pro trials — check your email.

**What happens to my data if I downgrade?**

All your data is preserved. You'll have read-only access to bookmarks beyond the Free limit until you upgrade again or delete some.

**Can I switch billing cycles?**

Yes, contact support and we'll prorate your subscription.
