## FAQ

**Is there a free trial for Pro?**

Your Free plan includes AI tokens to try Pro features. Occasionally we offer full Pro trials — check your email.

**What happens when my subscription expires?**

You keep all your data. Features beyond Free limits become read-only until you renew.

**Can I get a refund on Lifetime?**

Yes, within 7 days of purchase. After that, Lifetime purchases are final.

**Do prices include tax?**

Prices shown are before tax. Applicable sales tax or VAT added at checkout based on your location.

**Is there a team/business plan?**

Enterprise plans available for teams. Contact sales@navhub.info.
