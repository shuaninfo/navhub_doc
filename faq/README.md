# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about NavHub.

## Getting Started

### What is NavHub?

NavHub is a smart personal navigation platform that helps you organize bookmarks, build customized dashboards, and use AI to manage your online resources efficiently.

### Is NavHub free?

Yes! NavHub has a free plan with 500 bookmarks, 3 pages, and basic features. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited bookmarks and advanced features.

### How do I create an account?

1. Go to [navhub.info/signup](https://navhub.info/signup)
2. Sign up with email or use Google/GitHub/Apple
3. Verify your email
4. Start using NavHub

### Can I use NavHub on mobile?

Yes, NavHub is fully responsive and works on mobile browsers. Native mobile apps are in development.

## Bookmarks

### How many bookmarks can I save?

- **Free**: 500 bookmarks
- **Pro**: Unlimited

### Can I import from Chrome/Firefox?

Yes! Export your bookmarks as HTML from your browser and import them in NavHub Settings → Import/Export.

[Full import guide →](guides/import-bookmarks)

### How do I organize bookmarks?

Use a combination of:
- **Folders** — For hierarchy (Work, Personal, Projects)
- **Tags** — For cross-cutting topics (#to-read, #reference)

### What happens if I exceed the free limit?

Your bookmarks are preserved. You can view all, but can only add new ones if you delete some or upgrade to Pro.

## Widgets

### What widgets are available?

Free: Clock, Weather, Bookmarks, To-Do, Search, Notes

Pro adds: RSS Feed (unlimited), Embed, Calendar, Analytics

[Full widget list →](features/widgets)

### How do I add a widget?

1. Click **+ Add Widget** on your dashboard
2. Select the widget type
3. Configure settings
4. Position by dragging

### Can I resize widgets?

Yes, drag the corners or edges of most widgets to resize them.

## AI Features

### What AI features are included?

- Auto-categorization (suggest tags/folders)
- AI summaries (extract key points)
- Smart search (natural language)
- Translation

### How do AI tokens work?

AI features consume tokens. Free gets 10,000/month, Pro gets 100,000/month.

[AI token details →](features/ai)

### Are my bookmarks used for AI training?

No. Your data is never used to train AI models. It's only processed to provide features to you.

## Account & Billing

### How do I upgrade to Pro?

1. Go to Settings → Account
2. Click **Upgrade to Pro**
3. Choose billing cycle
4. Enter payment details

[Upgrade guide →](membership/upgrade)

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in Settings → Account. You keep Pro until the end of your billing period.

### What payment methods do you accept?

- Credit/Debit cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay

### Is there a refund policy?

Yes, 7-day money-back guarantee on first purchase, no questions asked.

## Privacy & Security

### Is my data secure?

Yes:
- All data encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
- Data stored encrypted at rest
- We don't sell or share your data

### Can I export my data?

Yes, export all your bookmarks anytime in Settings → Import/Export.

### Do you track my browsing?

No. The browser extension only activates when you click it to save a bookmark.

## Technical

### What browsers are supported?

- Chrome (and Chromium-based)
- Firefox
- Edge
- Safari (web app only, extension coming soon)

### Does it work offline?

Basic viewing works offline. Syncing requires internet connection.

### Is there an API?

Enterprise plans include API access. Contact sales@navhub.info.

## Troubleshooting

### I forgot my password

1. Go to [navhub.info/reset-password](https://navhub.info/reset-password)
2. Enter your email
3. Click the reset link in your email
4. Create a new password

### The extension isn't working

1. Make sure you're signed in
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Disable and re-enable the extension
4. Clear browser cache

### My bookmarks aren't syncing

1. Check your internet connection
2. Sign out and back in
3. Force refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R)
4. Contact support if issue persists

### I'm getting an error

Note the error message and contact support@navhub.info with:
- What you were trying to do
- The exact error message
- Your browser and version
